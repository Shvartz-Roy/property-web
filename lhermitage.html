01 / THE ARCHITECTURAL CONCEPT

Designed to blur the line between living space and ocean mist.

Perched 800 feet above the crashing surf of Highway 1, L'Hermitage was crafted by Olson Kundig architects as a monolithic haven of board-formed concrete, local travertine stone, and sweeping floor-to-ceiling motorized glazing.

Every space has been oriented toward the sea horizon—allowing light to shift organically across polished concrete floors throughout the day, ending in panoramic sunset views across the infinity pool terrace.
